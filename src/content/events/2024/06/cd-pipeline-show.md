The 2024 State of Continuous Integration and Continuous Delivery (CI/CD) Report, commissioned by the Continuous Delivery Foundation (CDF) and Cloudbees, and authored by SlashData, unveils the current landscape of the CI/CD developer ecosystem while exploring its evolution over the past two and a half years.

This comprehensive report looks at what proportion of developers are engaged in the broader DevOps space and examines how many work with CD technologies in particular.
