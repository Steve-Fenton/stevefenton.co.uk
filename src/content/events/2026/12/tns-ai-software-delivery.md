Two new datasets tell the same uncomfortable story. A Harvard study of 445 million work events found that AI-driven developer productivity gains mostly aren't reaching a company's actual output, in fact, most organizations convert their AI activity into only a modest delivery improvement, while a small group pulls dramatically ahead. The difference isn't how much code teams generate. It's what happens to that code on its way to customers.

In this session, Steve Fenton of Octopus Deploy digs into what the evidence says about high-performance software delivery in the AI era: why faster coding rarely means faster delivery, why code review isn't the fundamental constraint most teams assume, and what the small group seeing real gains do differently. He'll lay out a practical way to find your real bottleneck and move the gains from activity through to business outcomes.
