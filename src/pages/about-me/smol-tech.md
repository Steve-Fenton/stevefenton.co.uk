I've been on a tech diet and have been gradually moving my stuff to smaller companies who emphasize positive practices and behaviors. I've grouped all the switches. They made sense to me given my goals and, of course, you might make different choices.

Some moves come with an ethics-tax. They just aren't as smooth and easy as the big tech option. I'm happy to pay it. Even so, there are examples in here that are unbelievably better. Navidrome, StoryGraph, and Proton are far better than what they replaced.

I've fallen in love with my music collection again and I'm glad to have cobbled together 20 GB of music, though I've obviously lost a ridiculous number of records I bought on MP3 platforms that have gone, or all the CD, vinyl, and cassette tapes I couldn't justify space for when I had it all at the touch of a button.

:::div{.note}

The term <em>smol</em> refers to something adorably small. It comes from pet-talk, as in "Aw! Doggy is so smol". The idea behind my smol-ification is to start using tech I love again. I'm so done with tech that makes me sad.

:::
