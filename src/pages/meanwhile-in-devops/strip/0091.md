Blake is searching online for jobs. A big job ad appears that says, "Join our AI-FIRST dev team" and Blake hits the "Apply now" button.

Fast forward to day one of the new job...

"I'm struggling to find my desk," Blake tells his onboarding buddy. "Or a laptop, or the toilets."

"Your needs must wait," the onboarding buddy says. "As you know, we're AI-first."

## Meanwhile in DevOps

The *Meanwhile in DevOps* series is an ill-conceived and badly drawn comic strip about software delivery.
