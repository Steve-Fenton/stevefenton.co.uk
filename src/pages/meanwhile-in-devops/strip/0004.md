Murray and Arlo are sitting at their desks, which are next to each other. Murray is looking happy and a little vacant. He points at his screen.

"Check out this cool new feature," Murray says with excitement.

"Where are the tests? I thought we agreed to do TDD?" Arlo asks.

"We did," exclaims Murray. "I'll add those later."
