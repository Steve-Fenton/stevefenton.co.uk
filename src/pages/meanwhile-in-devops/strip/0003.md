Murray and Arlo bump into each other in the office.

"We shouldn't deploy on Friday," Murray tells Arlo, mostly on autopilot.

"Why not?" Arlo asks.

"It's bad for work-life balance" Murray parrots, having read something like that in an article.

Doug, who was listening in, suddenly joins the conversation, wielding his trusty clipboard.

"Fine," Doug says. "We'll move all deployments to weekends."

:::

You can find a longer critique of ["no Friday deployments" on The New Stack](https://thenewstack.io/deploy-on-friday-moratorium-doesnt-achieve-admirable-goal/).
