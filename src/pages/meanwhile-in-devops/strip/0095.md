Doug has rushed into the development team space in a panic. Waving a clipboard around, Doug addresses everyone loudly.

"It's the biggest discount sale of the year," he blurts. "And someone kicked off a deployment."

"It was me," Blake raises his hand.

"What?!" Doug exclaims. He points to a cork board covered in printouts on different coloured paper, tapping a note partly obscured by more recently added pages. "Didn't you read this memo?"

## Meanwhile in DevOps

The *Meanwhile in DevOps* series is an ill-conceived and badly drawn comic strip about software delivery.

I'm many things, but not an artist. It's doubtful whether I'm funny, too.
