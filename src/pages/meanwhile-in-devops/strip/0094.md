Arlo is finishing up an explanation of the PHP security issue.

"... and the secret key was just: 4", Arlo concludes.

"Oh my goodness," Murray exclaims. "That's why I use such a strong password!"

"Like what?" Arlo asks.

"0000000000000100."

<small>On a serious note, the PHP maintainers did a great job in getting a fix out for this. All the folks online saying, "I'm glad I moved off PHP," (as if they had predicted this CVE) are missing the point that whatever they've moved *to* also has CVEs, but doesn't make the mainstream headlines... because it's only part of the "other 30% of the Internet", not the 70% that PHP runs.</small>
