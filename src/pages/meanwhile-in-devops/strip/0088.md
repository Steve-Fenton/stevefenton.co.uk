Ellis is on screen presenting his latest webinar.

"Hello," he begins, pausing for a beat or two. "... welcome... to... today's... session... on... secure... software..."

Later that day, Blake bumps into Ellis in the corridor. He's confused about why Ellis was speaking so slowly on the webinar, but isn't sure whether to mention it. After a deep breath, Blake decides to ask.

"Why did you speak so slow on the webinar today?"

Ellis responds instantly.

"Everyone watches them back at double speed!"

:::

## Meanwhile in DevOps

The *Meanwhile in DevOps* series is an ill-conceived and badly drawn comic strip about software delivery.
