A giant spaceship approaches a lush planet in deep space. It makes a throbbing noise, even though sound can't travel in a vacuum. Suddenly, a blast of light bursts from the spaceship and makes contact in the planet's ocean, causing a great wave to form. After a minute, the intense energy explodes the planet in a brilliant flash.

Meanwhile on Earth, a scientist in an observatory sees the supernova and exclaims: "Wow!"

:::

Inspired by the [SN2026ahvu](https://www.wis-tns.org/object/2026ahvu) supernova discovered in November 2026.

## Meanwhile in DevOps

The *Meanwhile in DevOps* series is an ill-conceived and badly drawn comic strip about software delivery.

I'm many things, but not an artist. It's doubtful whether I'm funny, too.
