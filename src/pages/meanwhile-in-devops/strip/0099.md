A binfluencer is recording a video in Marazion, Cornwall with Saint Michael's Mount in the background. The cobble path to the island is visible.

"I'm here at Saint Michael's Mound in Cornwall," the binfluencer begins. "Look how low the water is. How can climate change be real?"

That evening, the binfluencer posts an update. The path is no longer visible and they look worried.

"Over the past four hours, the sea level has dramatically risen!"

Later that night, they post once again. The stars shine over the island and the cobble path, which is visible once more.

"I just don't understand what's happening here," the binfluencer wails.

:::

Inspired by the [Reuters fact check](https://www.reuters.com/fact-check/photos-britains-st-michaels-mount-are-not-evidence-sea-level-rise-is-trivial-2026-11-10/) on the misleading claim that the sea level around St Michael's Mount in Cornwall hasn't changed.
