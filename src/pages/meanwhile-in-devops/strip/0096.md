Arlo and Ellis are having a coffee in the breakout room. Arlo has a filter, no milk. Ellis has a caramel frappe with extra syrup and a squeeze of lime.

"Coding War Games found a 10x performance gap," Arlo explains. "But results clustered by orgs with better and quieter workspaces and fewer interruptions. So, investing in DevEx gets you that 10x, which is twice the median performance."

"What if," Ellis beings. "We ignore all that and just use more AI?"
