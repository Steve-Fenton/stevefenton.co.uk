After Allinson's ran a survey about Britain's favourite sandwiches and found people were using avocado, chillis, and olives, a boomer self-ignites into a rant in the office kitchen.

"Kids today want woke sandwiches," he spits. "I mean... What's wrong with good old ham and mustard."

"Why does this even matter to you?" Blake asks, slightly interrupting the flow of the rant.

"Because... I should be allowed to eat whatever sandwich I want!"
