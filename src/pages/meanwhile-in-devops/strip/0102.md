Arlo has found an old software package with a DVD on a shelf, covered in dust and cobwebs.

"What even is that software?" Arlo asks.

"No idea," Blake responds. "It's always been sat up there."

"It's probably nothing important," Arlo concludes.

18 months earlier, their boss is on the golf course taking a big swing. The VP of Enterprise Sales is leaning on his club while watching.

"It's pricey," the Sales VP explains. "But trust me, your team needs it!"
