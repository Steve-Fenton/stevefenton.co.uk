A city official in the pocket of dastardly tech is addressing a crowd. There's a protester in a Dead Kennedys t-shirt holding a placard that reads "FLOCK OFF".

"We have listened to your feedback," the city official begins. "It was wrong to spy on the population. All CivilSpy cameras will be removed... and be replaced with CivilShock auto-tasers!"

:::

Inspired by [stories](https://www.theguardian.com/us-news/2026/aug/20/flock-cameras-surveillance) of cities dropping Flock cameras due to public pressure, but immediately replacing them with products from Axon.

## Meanwhile in DevOps

The *Meanwhile in DevOps* series is an ill-conceived and badly drawn comic strip about software delivery.

I'm many things, but not an artist. It's doubtful whether I'm funny, too.
