It's ancient times and a rich bearded figure enters a shop.

"Good day, shop keeper," he booms. "I'm planning a great celebration. If you supply the food, you'll be rewarded with riches in gold."

"I'm sorry, King Midas," the shop keeper responds. "I can't get rid of the gold I already have. I don't want more of it."

In the background, piles of gold have been stacked in messy piles against the walls and in the next room.

:::
